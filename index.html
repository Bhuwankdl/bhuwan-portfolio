01 — Profile

Who I am

A finance-minded student who learns markets by working inside them, not just studying them.

Three years of hands-on stock market experience, a completed brokerage internship, and research presented on two continents — all before finishing the BBA.

I'm a BBA student at Valley State College (affiliated to Pokhara University) with over three years of personal experience following the stock market. I completed a three-month internship at Sani Securities Pvt. Ltd. (Stock Broker No. 42, licensed by SEBON), where I assisted in trade execution, analyzed NEPSE trends, and supported portfolio tracking for clients.

My academic work centers on how global macroeconomic forces move the Nepal Stock Exchange — a research paper on this topic has been presented at an international conference in Indore, India, and published in the Himalayan Journal of Multidisciplinary Research (HJMR).

Beyond the numbers, I take on leadership and mentoring roles: training peers on share-market basics, coordinating college events, and most recently serving as Event Manager for a Parliamentary Simulation of a Budget Speech and Q&A session — while also stepping into the debate itself as the role-played leader of a parliamentary party.
